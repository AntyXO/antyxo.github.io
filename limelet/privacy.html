Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-11-16

This policy covers the Limelet: File Converter app for iPhone and iPad.

The short version

  • Limelet converts your files on your device. They are never uploaded.
  • The developer collects no personal data and has no accounts or analytics.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob (unless you buy Limelet Unlimited). Google may collect some data for that, with your consent.
  • Apple handles purchases.

Your files

Conversion runs entirely on your device. Your documents, photos, audio and video are not sent to the developer or to any server. Converted files are saved in the app's folder on your device (visible in the Files app) until you delete them.

Data the developer collects

None. The developer does not collect, receive or store personal data. Limelet has no accounts or sign-in, and no analytics or crash-reporting tools of its own.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version of Limelet shows banner ads and optional rewarded ads from Google AdMob. Limelet Unlimited removes all ads.

Before showing personalized ads, the app asks for your choice using Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form where required, and Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can say no. You can change these choices later in the app's privacy options (where shown) and in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.

When ads are shown, Google may collect and use device identifiers (such as the advertising identifier, if you allow tracking), coarse location (approximated from your IP address), usage data (such as ad interactions) and diagnostics (such as crash and performance data). Google handles this data under its own policies. See How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

Purchases

Purchases of Limelet Unlimited are processed by Apple through the App Store. The developer does not see your payment details or Apple ID. Restoring purchases is done through Apple.

Children

Limelet is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone. Parents can use Screen Time and Apple's content restrictions to limit ads and purchases.

Your rights (GDPR and CCPA)

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct, delete or sell. If you are in the EEA, UK or California, you have rights over data held by Google; use your Google settings or Google's privacy tools to exercise them. Limelet does not sell personal information. You may also contact us with any privacy question.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
