Why Limelet

  • On-device. Conversion happens on your phone or tablet. Your files never leave it.
  • One tap. Pick a file and tap the format on the wheel.
  • No account. No sign-up, no login.
  • Works offline. Convert on a plane or without signal.
  • Made for iPhone and iPad, with Dark Mode, Dynamic Type, VoiceOver and Reduce Motion support.

What it converts

  • Word, Excel, PowerPoint, RTF, HTML, text and Markdown to PDF
  • PDF, RTF, text and HTML to Word (DOCX); PDF and Word to text
  • Photos between JPEG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF, GIF and more; several photos into one PDF
  • PDF merge, split, compress, and pages to images
  • Audio and video in formats Apple devices support
  • ZIP files: create and extract
