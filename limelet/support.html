Frequently asked questions

How does the free daily limit work?

Limelet includes 3 free conversions a day. The count resets daily. When you reach the limit you can buy Limelet Unlimited or wait until tomorrow.

What is Limelet Unlimited?

A one-time purchase (no subscription) that removes the daily limit, on every device signed in with your Apple ID. Find it on the limit screen that appears when you run out of free conversions.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open the limit screen and tap Restore Purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought with. If it does not work, check that you are signed in to the App Store in iOS Settings.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. Conversion happens on your device and works offline. Converted files are saved in the Files app under On My iPhone (or iPad) > Limelet > Converted.

Which formats are supported?
  • Documents to PDF: DOCX/DOC, XLSX/XLS, PPTX/PPT, RTF, HTML, TXT, Markdown
  • To Word: PDF, RTF, TXT, HTML to DOCX; PDF and DOCX to TXT
  • Images: JPEG, PNG, HEIC, TIFF, GIF, BMP and PDF, depending on your device; several images into one PDF
  • PDF: merge, split, compress, remove metadata, extract text, pages to images
  • Audio and video: MP4, MOV, M4V and Apple audio formats
  • ZIP: create and extract

Complex layouts may look different because Apple's built-in renderer is used. Word output keeps editable text, not tables or original page layout.
